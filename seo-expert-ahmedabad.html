// 09 · faq

Frequently asked questions

How much does SEO cost in Ahmedabad?

It depends on the size of your website, how competitive your keywords are and whether you need a one-time audit, a fix-it project or ongoing monthly work. Send your website and goals and I will give you a fixed quote after a short review.

How long does SEO take to show results?

Technical fixes and Google Business Profile improvements can show results within weeks. Competitive keywords usually take three to six months of consistent work. You get a report every month so you can see what is moving.

What do I get in an SEO audit?

A review of crawlability, indexing, speed and Core Web Vitals, redirects and broken links, sitemaps and robots.txt, schema markup, on-page SEO, content gaps and competitors, turned into a prioritised list of what to fix first.

Do you do local SEO for Google Maps?

Yes. I optimise your Google Business Profile, build consistent citations on directories such as Justdial, Sulekha, IndiaMART and Bing Places, and create service and location pages so you appear in Google Maps and "near me" searches.

Can you fix the problems as well as find them?

Yes. I am also a developer, so I can fix most technical issues myself, including on WordPress, instead of handing you a report to pass on to someone else.

Do you work with businesses outside Ahmedabad?

Yes. I work remotely with businesses across India and abroad. Most of my SEO experience so far is on US-based websites.

Are you available alongside your full-time job?

Yes. I work full-time as a Jr. AI Automation & SEO Executive and take on a limited number of freelance and remote SEO projects alongside that role.
