// 09 · faq

Frequently asked questions

What can AI automate for a small business?

Common examples are answering routine customer questions, capturing and sorting leads, drafting quotes and reports, extracting data from invoices and documents, and voice control of field or sales apps.

Which AI models and tools do you use?

Google Gemini (including Gemini Live for real-time voice), models served through Groq, Deepgram for speech-to-text, and other LLM APIs, with Flutter, React, Node.js, AWS Lambda, S3 and Supabase around them.

How long does it take to build an AI app?

A focused prototype can often be built in one to two weeks; a full product takes longer depending on scope. You get a timeline after a short scoping call.

Do you build mobile apps as well as web apps?

Yes. I build cross-platform mobile apps with Flutter and web apps with React and Node.js, both with AI features built in.

Will AI replace my staff?

That is not the goal. The best automations remove repetitive work so your team can spend time on customers and decisions, with a person reviewing anything important.

Is my business data safe?

Automations are built on your own cloud accounts where possible, share only the data a task needs, and keep India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 in mind for personal information.

Do you work remotely?

Yes. I am based in Ahmedabad and work remotely with clients across India and abroad.
