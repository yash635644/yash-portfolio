// 09 · faq

Frequently asked questions

How often should my business post on social media?

Consistency matters more than volume. For most small businesses, a few quality posts a week on the platforms your customers use is better than posting daily for a month and then stopping. I recommend a rhythm you can keep up.

Which social media platforms should my business use?

Instagram and Facebook suit most local businesses in Ahmedabad, Pinterest suits visual businesses such as food, interiors and fashion, and LinkedIn suits B2B and professional services. I recommend platforms based on where your customers actually are.

Do you create the designs and captions?

Yes. I plan the content, create the post designs, write the captions and hashtags, and schedule or publish the posts.

Does social media help SEO?

Social links are not a direct Google ranking factor, but active, consistent profiles help people find and trust your brand, send visitors to your website, and help Google and AI assistants connect your profiles to your business.

Do you run paid ads?

My focus is organic social media: planning, creating and publishing posts and managing profiles. I do not currently offer paid ad campaign management.

Can you manage social media alongside SEO?

Yes. Social media works best alongside SEO, and I can plan both together so your posts, profiles and website support each other.

Are you available alongside your full-time job?

Yes. I work full-time as a Jr. AI Automation & SEO Executive and take on a limited number of freelance and remote projects alongside that role.
