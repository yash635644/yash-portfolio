// 09 · faq

Frequently asked questions

What is GEO (Generative Engine Optimization)?

GEO is the practice of making a website and brand easy for generative AI tools like ChatGPT, Gemini and Perplexity to understand and cite. It combines technical SEO, structured data, clear entity information and answer-first content.

What is AEO (Answer Engine Optimization)?

AEO is structuring content so answer engines, voice assistants and AI Overviews can pull a direct, accurate answer from your page. Clear questions, short direct answers and FAQ schema are central to it.

Does llms.txt help my website rank in AI search?

llms.txt is a proposed standard that gives AI tools a clean summary of your site. Support across AI platforms is still limited, so I treat it as a low-cost extra on top of structured data and good content, not a replacement for them.

How long does it take to get cited by ChatGPT or Gemini?

It varies. Assistants that search the live web can pick up changes within weeks once your pages are crawlable and clear. Answers based on model training data change more slowly. Consistent signals across the web speed things up.

Is AI SEO different from normal SEO?

It builds on normal SEO. Crawlability, speed and quality content still come first. AI SEO adds structured data, entity consistency, answer-first formatting and AI-crawler access on top.

Can you check what ChatGPT says about my business?

Yes. The AI visibility audit includes checking how major AI assistants describe your business and competitors, which sources they cite, and where the information gaps are.

Can you guarantee my business will be cited by AI?

No one can honestly guarantee that, because AI tools decide their own answers. What I can do is remove the blockers and give AI tools clear, consistent, trustworthy information to work with.
